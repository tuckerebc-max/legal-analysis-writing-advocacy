# Construct and source ledger

**Registry:** `EXP-LEGAL` / `COMP-LEGAL-REASONING`  
**Design status:** `READY_WITH_CONDITIONS`  
**Project snapshot:** 2026-08-11

## Construct boundary

Legal reasoning is a professional specialization of argumentation and writing: identify issues, research and evaluate authority and facts, apply rules or standards to facts, draft for a defined audience, disclose uncertainty, and act within ethical and role boundaries. It is not legal advice, licensure, or general debate.

## Release conditions

Close current NCBE constructed-response/scoring capture, jurisdictional variation, professional-to-secondary transfer evidence, and rights/ethical handling. Use fictional/public supplied authority and qualified legal review for consequential applications.
